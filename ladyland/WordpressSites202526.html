A participatory theatre production inspired by Sultana’s Dream, the iconic short-story by Rokeya Sakhawat Hosein. Imagining Ladyland 121 years after Rokeya, the story invites the audience to decide if to grant men night rights or not.

Produced by HerStory Foundation with the support of UNDP and AUS. Originally created with the assistance of the British Council’s Women of the World grant 2026.
